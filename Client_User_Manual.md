# 🏥 Medical ERP & Barcode System - User Manual

Welcome to your new Medical ERP System! This software has been custom-built to provide a premium, fast, and highly secure experience for managing your pharmacy or medical shop.

This guide will help you understand and use all the features of your software.

---

## 1. Dashboard (Home)
The Dashboard is the control center of your shop. When you open the software, you will see:
- **Today's Revenue**: Total money earned today.
- **Total Stock Value**: The estimated total MRP value of all medicines currently in your shop.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Shows items that have a quantity of less than 10 so you know when to order more.
- **Urgent Expiry Alerts (90 Days)**: Automatically warns you about medicines that are going to expire in the next 3 months (90 days). The date format is consistently **DD-MM-YYYY**.
- **Sales Graph**: A visual chart of your sales over the last 7 days.

---

## 2. Smart Purchase Entry (Adding New Stock)
Whenever new medicine arrives at your shop, you need to add it to the system.

**How to add stock:**
1. Click on **"Purchase Entry"** from the left menu.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Medicine Name** (e.g., Paracetamol 500mg)
   - **Batch No**, **Quantity**, and **Distributor / Agency**
   - **Purchase Date** & **Expiry Date**
   - **MRP ₹** & **Distributor Bill Number**
3. Click **"Save Entry & Generate Barcode"**.
4. The system will automatically save the medicine and instantly generate a unique Barcode at the bottom of the screen.

---

## 3. Inventory Management & Printing Barcodes
This section allows you to view your entire stock, edit mistakes, or print barcode stickers for your medicine boxes.

**How to manage and print:**
1. Go to **"Inventory"** from the menu.
2. You will see a list of all medicines. You can use the **Search Bar** to find medicines by Name, Batch, or Barcode ID.
3. **To Edit/Delete**: Hover over a medicine card and click the Edit (Pencil) or Delete (Trash) icon.
4. **To Print a Single Barcode**: Click the **"Print Single Label"** button on any medicine card.
5. **To Bulk Print**: 
   - Check the small box on the top left of the medicine cards you want to print.
   - Click the black **"Print (X)"** button at the top.
   - A window will open where you can type how many **Copies** you want for each medicine.
   - Click **"Start Print"**. Your thermal printer (50x25mm labels) will print them perfectly!

---

## 4. Quick Sell (Billing & Scanning)
This is the Point of Sale (POS) screen where you bill your customers.

**How to bill a customer:**
1. Click on **"Quick Sell"**.
2. **Scan the Barcode**: Take your barcode scanner and scan the label on the medicine. The item will be instantly added to the cart!
3. *(Alternative)* **Manual Search**: If the barcode scanner is not working, you can manually type the medicine name in the "Search Medicine" box and click it to add.
4. **Adjust Quantity**: In the cart (right side), you can click `+` or `-` to increase/decrease the quantity of the medicine the customer is buying.
5. **Checkout**: The total amount will be calculated automatically. Once the customer pays, click **"Complete Sale"**. The stock will be automatically deducted from your inventory.

---

## 5. Reports & Analytics
To see how your business is performing and track distributors.

1. Go to **"Reports"**.
2. You can view **Today's Profit / Revenue** and the total **Bills Generated**.
3. **Distributor Performance**: Check which distributor is making you the most money and how much stock is left from them.
4. **Today's Sold Items**: A detailed list of exactly what was sold today.
5. **90 Days Expiry & Low Stock**: A full, printable list of items that need your attention.

---

## ⚙️ General Tips & Troubleshooting
- **Date Format**: The software automatically handles dates in the Indian Standard format **DD-MM-YYYY** (Day-Month-Year) everywhere on the printed labels and lists.
- **Refresh Button**: If you feel the data on the screen is old, click the **"Refresh"** button at the top right of the screens to instantly fetch the latest data.
- **Printing Issues**: Ensure your Thermal Printer is set as the default printer in Windows and the paper size is correctly set to **50mm x 25mm** in the printer properties.
